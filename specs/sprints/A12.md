# Sprint A12 — Event-driven follow-up

> **Status:** IMPLEMENTED (awaiting review) · spec v1 · 2026-10-19 — see **As built** at the end
> **Parent docs:** [`SCOPE-GAP-PLAN.md`](SCOPE-GAP-PLAN.md) (gaps 2 and 4; decision D4) · final scope §5C.4, §6, §7 ·
> [`A3.md`](A3.md) (stop-on-reply) · [`A11.md`](A11.md)
> **Outcome:** a reply no longer ends the conversation. Rebecca answers, then — if they go quiet — sends **one**
> follow-up two days later (D4), within the same caps. A bounce or a wrong number stops only that channel; the
> other permitted channel carries on. Follow-up e-mails respect the person's working hours, and a campaign can
> have an end date.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | After a reply Rebecca answered (`answered`) or sent the booking link for (`link_sent`), the old plan stops (as before) and **one follow-up e-mail** is planned **2 days** later (goal `follow_up_after_reply`, same campaign). A newer reply replaces it (it stops on reply too), so it is always "one follow-up after the *last* reply". The usual policy applies: consent, do-not-contact, gap, the per-person touch cap | Scope §7: event-driven, multi-turn, no fixed 4-email sequence — but no endless chasing (D4) |
| F2 | Replies that **don't end** the conversation keep the plan: out-of-office (snooze, as before), "no calls" / "no e-mails" (that channel's steps cancelled, the rest stays — fixes A11's stop-then-block) | Scope §6 "update channel preferences and block the disallowed channel" |
| F3 | **Bounce → that channel only**: a bounced address (DSN reply or SendGrid event) cancels the plan's e-mail steps and suppresses the address; call steps continue if permitted. A call ending **wrong number** suppresses that number and cancels call steps; e-mail continues. If nothing is left, the plan stops (`no_channel_left`). Complaints and "don't call me again" still stop everything | Scope §6 "continue through another channel only if permitted" |
| F4 | **E-mail working hours**: `agent_settings.email_window_start/end` (default 08:00–18:00, contact-local). Outside it the gate defers the step (`OUTSIDE_EMAIL_WINDOW`, no attempt used). Not applied to replies, nor to the **first e-mail to an inbound lead** (they just asked — speed matters) nor when the person's timezone is unknown | Scope §7 "working-hour rules" for no-response follow-ups |
| F5 | **Campaign end date**: `outreach_campaigns.ends_at` (admin sets it on the Campaigns screen). The 5-minute housekeeping job ends past-date campaigns (status `completed`) and stops their active plans (`campaign_ended`) | Scope §7 "an overall campaign stop date" |
| F6 | Booking conflicts: already re-checked at confirm time on calls (L6) and at booking time for e-mail (slot recomputed + `SLOT_UNAVAILABLE` on a race) — A14 proves it end to end | Scope §14 |

## Data (migration 0076)
`agent_settings.email_window_start time NOT NULL DEFAULT '08:00'`, `email_window_end … '18:00'`;
`outreach_campaigns.ends_at timestamptz NULL`.

## API
`PUT /admin/outreach/settings` takes the e-mail window; `PATCH /admin/outreach/campaigns/{id}` `{ends_at}` (admin);
the campaigns list returns `ends_at`.

## Tests
Integration: question answered → one follow-up 2 days out in the same campaign; a second reply replaces it; the
touch cap still holds; "no calls" keeps the e-mail steps; bounce keeps the call; wrong number keeps e-mail; a
step at 22:00 local waits for 08:00 without using an attempt; the inbound first e-mail is exempt; a campaign past
its end date stops its plans; tenant B can't set A's end date. Unit: window helpers.
