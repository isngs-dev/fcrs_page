> **Parent docs:** [`SCOPE-GAP-PLAN.md`](SCOPE-GAP-PLAN.md) (gaps 3–4, decision D5) · final scope §4 "conversation

> **Outcome:** every agent knows the person's story — what they asked in the chat, on a form, by e-mail and on a
> call, what we told them, what they objected to and what's next — and Rebecca handles "contact me later",
> "don't call me", "don't e-mail me", "move / cancel my meeting" and "not interested" the way the scope asks.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | **Lead memory** = one record per contact (`contact_memory`), a bounded list (last 40) of short, code-written items: `asked`, `need` (form answers), `objection`, `we_said`, `booked`, `summary` (call), each with channel and time. Written by code from facts we already hold — **no extra model calls** | Scope §4: a structured summary, "do not rely only on a long transcript"; deterministic and cheap |
| M2 | **Rendered on read** into one block: their chat questions (the existing chat summary, moved here from the composer), the memory items, **permission now** (per channel, incl. do-not-contact) and the **next planned step**. Every agent prompt gets it **framed as data, not instructions** (same `<<< >>>` framing as e-mail replies) | One view for every channel; injection-safe |
| M3 | Readers: the follow-up composer, the e-mail answerer, and the voice answerer on calls | §6 step 3 "Build context" |
| M4 | Writers: form intake (answers → `need`), e-mail replies (their words → `asked` / `objection`; our answer → `we_said`; bookings → `booked`), calls (caller questions → `asked`, the call summary → `summary`, bookings → `booked`) | Every channel feeds the same memory |
| M5 | New e-mail reply intents (classifier labels): **`contact_later`** — a date they give (≤ 180 days) or, if none, 14 days → the plan restarts from that date and they get a short acknowledgement; **`no_calls`** → their numbers go on the do-not-call list (calls only), acknowledged; **`no_emails`** → their address is suppressed for e-mail only, no reply sent; **`reschedule_meeting`** → their next booking is moved to the time they propose if it's free (old one cancelled, reminders moved), else the booking link / a person; **`cancel_meeting`** → their next booking is cancelled and confirmed | Scope §6 table, §8 rescheduling/cancellation |
| M6 | **Not interested** (e-mail reply or call outcome) → a lasting **do-not-contact** entry (reason `not_interested`, channel `all`, their e-mail and phone) — visible in the do-not-contact list, removable by an admin (D5) | Scope: "Stop the relevant outreach immediately … do not try to persuade them" |
| M7 | Export/erase (A9) include the memory | Compliance §6 |

## Data (migration 0075)
`contact_memory(tenant_id, contact_id, items jsonb, updated_at)` (FK contacts, cascade);
`suppression_list.reason` also allows `not_interested`.

## Tests
Unit: memory rendering + trimming; the framing; label set. Integration: a form answer and a call question show
up in the next e-mail answer's prompt; contact_later with/without a date reschedules; no_calls blocks calls but
not e-mail; no_emails blocks e-mail only; reschedule moves the booking and its reminders, a taken slot doesn't;
cancel cancels + skips reminders; not interested (e-mail and call) blocks every channel and an admin can remove
it; memory is tenant-isolated and erased with the contact.
